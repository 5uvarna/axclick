How to open axclick
===================

1. Drag axclick into your Applications folder.

2. Open it. macOS says "axclick" Not Opened, because axclick isn't
   notarized by Apple yet. Click Done (not Move to Bin).

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security,
   and click Open Anyway next to the message about axclick.

4. Enter your Mac password and click Open.

axclick then asks for Accessibility access. Turn it on, and press
Command-Shift-Space in any app to see the labels.


Skip all of this next time
--------------------------

Paste this into Terminal to install or update axclick without the
warning. It downloads the latest version, checks its signature and
opens it:

    curl -fsSL https://5uvarna.github.io/axclick/install.sh | bash
